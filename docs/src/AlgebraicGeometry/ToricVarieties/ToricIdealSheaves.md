# Toric Ideal Sheaves

Let $X_\Sigma$ be a normal toric variety with Cox ring $S$. A homogeneous
ideal $I\subseteq S$ determines an ideal sheaf on $X_\Sigma$, constructed by
`ideal_sheaf(X, I)`.

The construction is currently supported when every maximal cone of the fan
$\Sigma$ has dimension equal to the rank of its ambient lattice $N$.
Equivalently, $\Sigma$ is **pure and full-dimensional** in
$N_\mathbb{R}$. This implies that $X_\Sigma$ has no torus factor, but is a
stronger condition. Neither smoothness nor completeness is required; in
particular, affine toric varieties satisfying the condition are supported.

The homogeneous ideal must be defined in `cox_ring(X)`. Its homogeneity is
checked when the graded ideal is constructed.

**Implementation note.** For an affine variety defined by a cone `C`, use
`normal_toric_variety(C)` to obtain a `NormalToricVariety`. The specialized
`AffineNormalToricVariety` representation (returned by
`affine_normal_toric_variety(C)`) is not accepted by this constructor. This
is a restriction of the current interface, not a restriction to non-affine
varieties.

Construction is lazy: the ideal on an affine chart is computed when its
restriction is first requested. This involves dehomogenizing the Cox-ring
equations and computing a kernel in the chart coordinate ring, which can be
expensive.

For example, let $X=\mathbb{P}^2$ and consider the conic defined by
$f=x^2-yz$. The corresponding ideal sheaf restricts to
$(u^2-v)$, $(uv-1)$, and $(u^2-v)$ on the three standard affine charts,
respectively, using their respective local coordinates.

## Constructors

```@docs
ideal_sheaf(X::NormalToricVariety, I::MPolyIdeal)
ideal_sheaf(td::ToricDivisor)
```

A cone $\tau\in\Sigma$ also determines the ideal sheaf of its orbit closure
$V(\tau)$, via `ideal_sheaf(X, tau)`. The corresponding Cox ideal is
generated by the variables indexed by rays of $\tau$. For the zero cone
$\tau=\{0\}$, this is the zero ideal sheaf.

```@docs
ideal_sheaf(X::NormalToricVariety, tau::Cone)
```
