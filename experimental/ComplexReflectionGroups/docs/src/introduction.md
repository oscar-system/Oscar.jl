# [Introduction](@id reflection-groups-introduction)

A complex reflection group is a finite group of linear transformations generated by
elements that fix a hyperplane pointwise. This component lets you work both with its
**type**, a conjugacy class in $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb C)$, and with **concrete matrix
realizations** over exact number fields. You specify the type when constructing a group;
recognizing the type of an arbitrary matrix group is a separate problem and is not
implemented here.

## What you can do

* Construct types from the Shephard--Todd classification, including direct products and
  trivial summands, and query their orders, degrees, codegrees, and reflection counts
  without constructing matrices. See [Types and classification](@ref crg-types) and
  [Polynomial invariants](@ref crg-invariants).
* Choose matrix models suited to the computation: standard-unitary Lehrer--Taylor
  models, CHEVIE models, or Magma models. Work with their exact coefficient fields,
  including readable relative towers and absolute simple presentations. See
  [Matrix realizations](@ref crg-models) and the complete list of
  [Lehrer--Taylor models](@ref crg-lt-models).
* Compute reflecting hyperplanes, their group orbits, and conjugacy classes of
  reflections. Their type-level labels are shared by the built-in models; their
  representatives are matrices in the chosen realization. See
  [Hyperplane orbits and reflection classes](@ref crg-orbits).
* Extract roots, coroots, nontrivial eigenvalues, and fixed hyperplanes from individual
  reflections, or construct a reflection from these data. See
  [Roots and coroots](@ref crg-roots).

## A first computation

Start with the reducible type $G_{33} \times G(8,4,6)$. Its basic invariants can be
queried without constructing any matrices; then construct a concrete matrix realization
of the same type:

```jldoctest
julia> T = complex_reflection_group_type([33, (8, 4, 6)])
Complex reflection group type G(8,4,6) x G33

julia> order(T)
2446118092800

julia> rank(T)
11

julia> number_of_reflections(T)
171

julia> complex_reflection_group(T)
Matrix group of degree 11
  over cyclotomic field of order 24
```

The [complex reflection group manual](@ref crg-manual) develops the theory alongside
examples, starting with individual reflections and proceeding to classification,
arithmetic models, and the organization of reflection data. Lehrer and Taylor's
*Unitary Reflection Groups* [LT09](@cite) is the main mathematical reference.

[Symplectic reflection groups](@ref srg-manual) form a separate subject. General support
for them is not yet implemented; the preliminary section documents only the cotangent
doubling of a complex reflection group. The reflection data developed here are also
intended for subsequent constructions of rational Cherednik algebras; those algebras
are not implemented by this component.
